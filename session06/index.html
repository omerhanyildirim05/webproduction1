Limbus Company

Limbus Company is a free-to-play, turn-based strategy and role playing video game developed and published by the South Korean studio Project Moon. Explore the city as the clock headed manager Dante, leading a bus full of 12 sinners to collect glowing relics known as Golden Boughs. Encounter eldritch horrors, deep philosopical narratives and delve deep into the inferno through the "Cantos" and "Deviaziones".

Undertale

The indie role-playing game Undertale centers on a human child who falls into the Underground, a subterranean world where monsters were banished long ago by humans through a long war. Control the eigth human child to fall into the underground, Frisk. Armed with determination, all of your actions impact this world, so choose your choices wisely. Good luck... partner :).

The Binding of Isaac

The Binding of Isaac is a 2D action-adventure roguelike video game created by Edmund McMillen and Florian Himsl. The story follows Isaac, a young boy who escapes into the basement of his home after his mother heras a voice from God commanding her to sacrifice him. The game takes places in a dark, disturbing world filled with monsterrs, grotesque creatures, and symbolic imagery inspired by religion, guilt and Isaac's fears.
